
LEADONSPOT - PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE

==================================================
PRODUCT 1: LEADONSPOT SMART CRM
==================================================

OVERVIEW

Leadonspot Smart CRM is an AI-enabled revenue management platform that helps businesses manage leads, opportunities, sales pipelines, customer relationships, and follow-up activities.

CORE FEATURES

1. Lead Management and Tracking
   Helps businesses organize and track their sales leads.

2. AI-Powered Lead Scoring
   Helps sales teams identify and prioritize high-potential leads.

3. Sales Pipeline Management
   Helps track opportunities through different sales stages.

4. Lead Decay Detection
   Identifies leads that may be losing engagement or becoming inactive.

5. AI-Generated Sales Pitch Suggestions
   Provides sales pitch suggestions to help sales representatives communicate with prospects.

6. Revenue Forecasting
   Helps businesses understand and forecast their sales performance.

7. Task and Follow-up Management
   Helps sales teams organize tasks and follow-up activities.

8. Bulk Lead Upload
   Allows users to upload multiple leads.

9. Duplicate Lead Detection
   Helps identify duplicate lead records.

10. Custom Fields and Role-Based Access
    Supports customized data fields and access based on user roles.

11. Workflow Automation
    Automates repetitive sales activities using triggers and actions.

12. AI Lead Agent
    Provides lead-level insights and recommended next actions.

WORKFLOW AUTOMATION EXAMPLES

- Automatically assign new leads to sales representatives.
- Create follow-up tasks when leads become inactive.
- Send welcome emails to newly registered leads.
- Notify managers when opportunities become stuck.
- Escalate leads requiring urgent attention.

AI CAPABILITIES

Smart CRM uses AI to help sales teams:

- Identify high-priority prospects.
- Understand lead activity.
- Identify potential deal risks.
- Determine the next best action.

WHO SHOULD USE SMART CRM?

- Sales teams
- Founders
- Business development teams
- Sales managers
- Growing businesses managing multiple leads and opportunities

- Identify relevant decision-makers.
- Prepare personalized sales pitches.
- Improve sales preparation.

It helps businesses communicate with prospects, qualify leads, and manage calling workflows.

CORE CAPABILITIES

- Inbound AI Calling.
- Outbound AI Calling.
- Automated New-Lead Calling.
- Lead Qualification.
- Conversational AI.
- Call Transcripts.
- Sentiment Analysis.
- CRM Synchronization.
- Smart Call Transfer.
- Automated Calling Workflows.

USE CASES

- Automatically call newly registered leads.
- Qualify incoming prospects.
- Conduct initial sales conversations.
- Handle routine customer inquiries.
- Transfer qualified prospects to human representatives.
- Maintain call records and summaries.

WHO SHOULD USE AI CALLING AGENT?

- Businesses handling large numbers of leads.
- Customer support teams.
- Businesses looking to automate calling workflows.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

Do not promise a particular number of calls, minutes, languages, integrations, or calling capacity without checking the current plan and configuration.
