
LEADONSPOT - PRICING AND SUBSCRIPTION INFORMATION

==================================================
GENERAL PRICING GUIDELINES
==================================================

Leadonspot offers different products with separate commercial arrangements.

PRODUCT PRICING STRUCTURE

1. Smart CRM
   Offers subscription plans with features and AI capabilities depending on the selected plan.

2. Nexus
   Separately priced product.

3. Data Intelligence
   Separately purchasable product with API capabilities.

4. AI Calling Agent
   Separate product with applicable calling and usage charges.

IMPORTANT PRICING RULES

- Always use the latest official pricing information.
- Never invent monthly or annual prices.
- Never invent discounts.
- Never promise free access to paid features.
- Never assume all four products are included in one subscription.
- Never promise unlimited usage.
- Exact prices, billing cycles, taxes, credits, usage limits and plan entitlements must be verified.

STANDARD PRICING RESPONSE

If a customer asks about pricing and exact information is unavailable, respond:

"Our pricing depends on the product and the features or usage you require. I can help you identify the right solution. Would you like our sales team to share the latest pricing details?"

==================================================
14-DAY FREE TRIAL
==================================================

Leadonspot Smart CRM offers a 14-day free trial.

TRIAL INCLUDES

- Up to 20 leads.
- 1 user.
- Bulk lead upload.
- Opportunities.
- Sales pipeline.
- Tasks.
- Data Intelligence Playground with 5 searches.

TRIAL LIMITATIONS

The trial does not include:

- Full Data Intelligence.
- AI Calling Agent access.

IMPORTANT RULES

- Do not promise that all premium features are available during the free trial.
- Do not describe the free trial as unlimited.
- Explain the trial limitations whenever relevant.
- Do not assume that other products are included in the Smart CRM trial.

No. The 14-day Smart CRM trial does not include AI Calling Agent access.

Question:

I don't have verified information about current discounts. Please contact the Leadonspot sales team for confirmation.
